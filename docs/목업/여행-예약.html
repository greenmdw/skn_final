Why this list

01A city festival overlaps your dates, so everything here is booked at least 14 days ahead.
02The hanbok shop you'd find first claims a 5-minute walk. 41 of 312 reviews say over 15. We swapped it for a shop 27 reviews put at 6 minutes.
03The workshop's "English guidance" claim did not hold, but it's the only meat-free-friendly slot left on your dates. Kept, with a printed Korean phrase card.
04The tour does refund for rain — but 9 reviews report three-week delays. Book it, don't count on a fast refund.
05Luggage storage hours are unverified. Two reviews mention them. We are not telling you it's open 24 hours.

근거의 근거다섯 문장 전부가 단계 03의 판정과 일대일로 연결된다. 설명이 스스로 지어낸 문장이 아니라는 것이 이 화면의 요지다 — 근거 문장을 생성하는 것과 그 문장을 믿을 수 있게 만드는 것은 다른 일이다.

Disclosure

How we use reviews

If reviews are filtered or used to rank, the basis has to be published. We make the obligation a screen.

Who may write
Only travellers with a confirmed booking. Anything else is used as evidence, never published as a review
What we read
Listing reviews · listing Q&A · public community posts with no advertising
When
Collected up to 2026-09-07 · last 24 months
Filtering
Reviews at or above 20% manipulation probability are excluded from the evidence sample. They are not deleted
Grading
We assign no star grade to a review. The only value a review produces is a verdict on a stated promise — confirmed, partly, refuted, or no evidence
Effect of a verdict
A verdict never removes a listing. Refuted items stay on the list with the warning attached
Ranking
Reviews never rank listings. They are used only to test whether a stated promise held
Excluded
94 of 865 (10.9%) — full distribution shown in stage 03
Translation
Machine-assisted. The Korean original is always shown beside it so you can check or ask someone
Retention
24 months from collection, then aggregates only
Deletion
On author request, or when the source post is removed. Past verdicts survive as counts only
Appeals
An operator can submit evidence to have a "refuted" verdict re-examined
